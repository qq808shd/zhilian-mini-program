# Built-in Image 2 — ui-mockup

Design an entirely new high-fidelity three-screen UI board for “知练”, a Chinese WeChat knowledge learning mini program. This is a cohesive finished MOBILE APP interface, not a wireframe. The previous designs were rejected for being ONLY text and thin outlined rectangles, inconsistent typography, dull colours and no real icons. Resolve those issues decisively. Screens side by side with white 390×844-like canvases, no device frame. Screen 1 home, Screen 2 learning with meaning revealed, Screen 3 study settings. Use a delicate pale pistachio / sage green style, clean and minimal, polished everyday consumer app. Crisp real-looking Chinese typesetting, rounded geometry, subtle organic green graphic accents. A coherent icon family is ESSENTIAL: rounded two-tone vector icons with pale filled backgrounds (open book with a sprout, rotate-arrow, pencil, gear/sliders, eye, circular degree icons and trash). Real pictographic symbols, not text glyphs masquerading as icons, not emoji. Icons at useful points only. Match stroke weights and icon bounding boxes. Do not use border outlines as the primary organization: combine tinted surfaces, white floating cards, gentle depth, spacing, and green filled controls. No large marketing illustrations, no massive blank cards, no redundant long copy.
Design system: very pale green background #F3F7F0, white surfaces, dark green main text #284A38, secondary #607366, primary forest #4D785B, light card green #E3EEDD, brighter small accent #9CBD85. SAME typography scale across all 3 screens: main section title 20px semibold, word title 30px semibold, body 16px regular with 1.7 line-height, labels 13px medium, button text 16px medium. Main action 48px tall with ~16px corner radius; rating actions 48px tall at bottom with equal widths, all visible, comfortable safe area. No gratuitous black text, no washed-out microtext. Navy/blue forbidden; destructive reset uses restrained muted rust text only.
Home: native title “知练”; top scope card taking ~220px with top small book icon badge and “当前学习”, “三字词” title and compact “切换” with arrows icon. At right an elegant small two-tone open-book/sprout pictogram as visual anchor (about70px, not stock art). Show “已学 20 / 113” with progress bar; below a four-part degree summary with SMALL real icons and labels/counts: “陌生 4”“了解 8”“掌握 6”“熟知 2”, not four ugly outlined buttons. Below a white study area “今日学习” with accessible gear icon on right. Two tactile FILLED/TINTED action tiles in one row: book-plus icon “新学” and number10, rotate-arrow icon “复习” and number6. Text “今日已学 2” kept small and quiet if needed. Below a slim white free-practice entry with pencil icon in green square, “自由练习” and small arrow. Four tabbar icons/labels “学习”“练习”“复习”“我的”.
Learning: native back arrow with centered “三字词”; a single compact progress row “1 / 10” with progress track. A white softly rounded card with small degree chip with icon “陌生”, quiet “未学习”, overflow dots. Word centered “晴雨表”, a subtle small leaf divider or tinted separator. Definition in very soft green content panel with tiny openbook glyph and title “释义”, body “能及时反映事物变化的指示物。” Below plain example text label “例句”, body “居民消费价格指数（CPI）是民生的‘晴雨表’。” Compact spacing and ample readability. No source filenames, no duplicate headers, no instructional paragraphs. Three EQUAL bottom beautiful tappable buttons with REAL circular icons and exact labels “陌生”“了解”“掌握”. Each only icon+label in one row. Neutral soft grey/sage/forest filled, not empty outlined boxes. Show all 3 on-screen. The hidden meaning version (to be implemented, no fourth screen required) has an eye icon and tap-to-reveal control in the card; preserve its space naturally rather than a giant empty block.
Settings: native back header “学习设置”. A pale green context strip with book icon “当前范围” and “三字词”, NO module/category selector, no broad module names, no multiple dropdowns. A white rounded quota card with small calendar/book-plus icon “每天新学”; big but consistent number “10” and unit “个” in center. Elegant green slider below from1to113, or minus and plus controls around number plus discreet slider; choose a clean solution with easy precision. Brief caption “保存后生效”. A separate data row with a subdued trash pictogram “清空学习记录” and secondary short text “学习进度与复习安排将重置”, chevron. Destructive row is NOT a giant red button. Bottom a green FILLED “保存设置” button with a small check icon. The reset needs an explicit confirmation dialog in the implemented app; no reset happens merely opening settings.
Result should look like one professional modern calm green product with a recognizable pictographic language, meticulous typography and strong hierarchy, very different from bare text-and-outline forms. No numbered screen captions outside the app, no explanatory annotations, no watermarks.
